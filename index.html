Day 1

BILLIONS

Build a colony. Fortify it. Survive 30 days of the dead.

Works offline. On your phone: Share → Add to Home Screen.

How to Play

Goal: keep the Command Center alive for 30 days. Hordes attack on days 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and a final one on day 30 — each from the direction the scouts announce, at night.

Build: tap a building in the bottom bar, tap the map to position it, then tap ✓ Build (or tap the ghost again). Walls place instantly on each tap; use Line to draw a wall between two taps.

Economy: Tents add workers and gold but eat food. Farms make food on open grass, Sawmills need forest, Quarries need rock. The yield preview shows what a spot earns.

Defense: Walls block zombies, Ballistas shoot over them. Barracks train Rangers and Soldiers. Tap a unit then tap the ground to move it; Army selects everyone.

Danger: gunfire attracts nearby zombies. If a tent is overrun, its colonists turn into zombies inside your walls!

Camera: drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
